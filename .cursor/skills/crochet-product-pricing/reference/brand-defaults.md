# Diana's art brand defaults

Same content as repo `docs/pricing/dianas-art-context.md` when working outside the shop repo.

- **Diana's art • made in Flagstaff** — handmade crochet, Flagstaff, AZ.
- **Sales:** local pickup, Venmo `@loopedlocal`, Mountainaire General Store display. **No shipping.**
- **Buyers:** gift givers, parents, hosts, tourists, country-store impulse.
- **Pricing:** Flagstaff in-person target; Etsy is reference, not default retail.
- **Yarn:** Michaels **Charisma** (stockings, hats, elf hat) vs **Bobbiny Premium 5mm** (coasters, hotpads, wine holders). Bobbiny is much more expensive per project.
- **Tax in cost floor:** Flagstaff combined sales tax on **supplies** (yarn + tag), not labor. Customer Venmo sales may not collect tax.
