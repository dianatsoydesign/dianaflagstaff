# Free GIFs and short animations

Places to get motion for the shop. Saved so we do not have to hunt for this again.

Prefer a short loop over a GIF when you can. Lottie files and muted video stay sharp and load lighter.

## Short animations

Use these first for the website.

| Site | Good for | Link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LottieFiles | Small looping motions (icons, loaders, little decorations). Download the free files marked for commercial use. They play as Lottie, not GIF. | https://lottiefiles.com/free-animations |
| Lordicon | Animated icons for buttons and small moments. The free set is fine. | https://lordicon.com/ |
| Mixkit | Short free video clips you can loop. A muted MP4 or WebM looks better than a GIF and is usually a smaller file. | https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/ |

## GIFs

| Site | Good for | Link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pixabay | Free GIFs you can use on the shop. | https://pixabay.com/gifs/ |
| Pexels | Mostly video, same idea. A short clip can become a loop. | https://www.pexels.com/ |

## Skip

Random GIF search results, and most of Giphy and Tenor. Those are fine for chat. The license often does not cover a store.

## Before you download

On each file, check that it says free for commercial use, and that you do not have to show their logo.
