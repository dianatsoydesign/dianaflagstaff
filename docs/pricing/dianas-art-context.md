# Diana's art :  pricing context

**Brand:** Diana's art • made in Flagstaff (handmade crochet, Flagstaff, AZ).

**Sales model:** Local only :  no shipping. Payment via Venmo (`@loopedlocal`). Pickup and friends/family; display at Mountainaire General Store area (country-store gift buyer).

**Buyer types:** Gift givers (holiday, housewarming, wine host), parents (elf hat, kid hats), locals dressing for cold mornings, tourists taking home a mountain-town handmade piece, impulse gift at a country store.

**Pricing stance:**

- Recommend **in-person Flagstaff** prices, not “Etsy item + shipping.”
- Etsy and national online listings are **reference**; Flagstaff/local and peer mountain-town comps matter more when available.
- **Bobbiny Premium 5mm** (coasters, hotpads, wine holders) is **much more expensive** yarn than **Michaels Charisma** (stockings, hats, elf hat). Never cost Bobbiny like Charisma.
- Venmo hobby sales may not charge customers tax; **Flagstaff sales tax on supplies** still belongs in **your cost floor** when buying yarn/tags at local retail.

**Product names:** Sentence case in catalog and copy (see repo rule `.cursor/rules/sentence-case.mdc`).
